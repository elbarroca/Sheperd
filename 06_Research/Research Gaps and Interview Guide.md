## Founder interview — Avi

Ask for artifacts or demonstrations, not adjectives.

1. What exact legal entity contracts, invoices, employs, owns the domain/code, and receives the fee?
2. Who are every founder, employee, contractor, advisor, investor, and accountable owner; what can each decide?
3. What works today end to end without roadmap language? Demonstrate manual service, MVP, checks, portal, filing, tracking, and reconciliation.
4. Show the check catalog: deterministic versus human judgment, test coverage, exception path, and owner.
5. Show a de-identified case ledger: customers, invoices, disputes, outcomes, recovery form, cycle, permission, losses, and no-decisions.
6. Which public claims will you remove, substantiate, or submit for reviewer approval?
7. Why does the current diagnostic use volume × $75 while ignoring port/spend, and who approved the model?
8. What does “recovered” mean for cash, credit, waiver, cancellation, expired credit, clawback, and success-fee timing?
9. What authority, hours, budget, compensation, systems, data access, and decisions will Michael receive?
10. What current funding, runway, cap-table, hiring, insurance, IP, and vendor evidence can be admitted?

## Product/data interview

1. Walk one permitted synthetic or de-identified case from intake to realized outcome.
2. Which inputs are required; which are optional; what makes a submission incomplete, rejected, or accepted?
3. What is automated, rule-based, AI-assisted, or manual at each step?
4. Which tariffs, contracts, carrier portals, APIs, crawlers, datasets, and licenses are used?
5. What are throughput, active minutes, wait time, backlog, exception rate, correction rate, and result SLA by case archetype?
6. How are check versions, evidence provenance, reviewer decisions, and outcome changes logged?
7. Where does D0–D3 data flow; which vendors, regions, identities, and retention controls apply?
8. What is the rollback path when a rule, model, source, or integration is wrong?
9. What happens after a carrier issues cash, credit, waiver, cancellation, or rejection?
10. Which product claims are current service, current MVP, pilot-only, or roadmap?

## Domain/legal interview

1. Who owns current-rule review, case review, marketing substantiation, and filing/settlement decisions?
2. Confirm exact Part 541 timing, current §541.4 status, liability-basis boundary, and reissue implications.
3. Define the decision tree separating direct dispute, Charge Complaint, formal/small claim, CADRS, and counsel.
4. What evidence and review are required before saying invalid, unlawful, eligible, recoverable, or expected recovery?
5. What accrual, OSRA applicability, contract/tariff, injury, causation, and remedy questions must stay human-only?
6. Which public sentences are approved, for what channel, until what date, and under what constraints?
7. What authority or customer consent is required for disputes, filings, case studies, and data handling?
8. What incident or error requires stopping a workflow or withdrawing a claim?

## Security/privacy interview

1. Which entity is controller/processor; what purposes and legal/contractual bases apply?
2. Which systems and subprocessors receive lead, CRM, invoice, shipment, and case data?
3. What regions, cross-border transfers, DPAs, standard clauses, and customer restrictions apply?
4. Show identity, least privilege, MFA, encryption, logs, vulnerability, incident, backup, and offboarding controls.
5. What are retention, deletion, litigation-hold, export, and customer-request procedures?
6. How do Typeform autosave and partial responses flow; which integrations are configured?
7. What D2 tools are enterprise-approved; what exact written conditions would permit D3 AI?
8. Who accepts data, approves exceptions, and owns privacy/security incidents?

## Customer interview guide — only after activation

Do not schedule or conduct these interviews while the activation gate is blocked.

1. Who owns D&D payment, disputes, carrier relationships, and ledger reconciliation?
2. Where do invoices, tariffs/contracts, free time, appointments, holds, closures, and return evidence live?
3. Which step consumes the most active time; which step waits longest; what rework recurs?
4. What prevents secure submission: authority, security review, workload, data fragmentation, value uncertainty, or timing?
5. How do cash, credits, waivers, cancellations, and expired credits appear in finance systems?
6. What proof, audit trail, approval, and workload reduction would justify a success fee?
7. What outcome would damage trust even if recovery exists?

## Partner interview guide — only after activation

1. Which importer problems are observed directly and how often?
2. What permission exists to introduce the account, and who owns the relationship?
3. What makes a referral qualified, accepted, rejected, or conflicted?
4. What stage visibility can be shared without customer data leakage?
5. What economics, attribution, exclusivity, and non-solicit terms are expected?
6. Which delivery failure would damage the partner relationship?
7. What comparable referral sample is needed before calling the channel effective?

## Stop rules

- Three failed authoritative source attempts → mark `unknown`; do not infer a negative.
- Missing owner, permission, secure path, review gate, measurable pilot, or rollback → block recommendation.
- Missing baseline, unit, formula, sample, source/assumption, or confidence → block numerical promotion.
- Written engagement, product truth, claims, or data-security gate open → no external readiness claim.
