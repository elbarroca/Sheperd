## Entity and authority diligence

Three materially different official-registry attempts were inconclusive. No negative conclusion is admissible. Evidence state: `unverified`; confidence: high that the search failed, low on entity state. Source: [[10_Sources/Source - SheperD Entity Search Attempts - 2026-07-15]].

Before Michael represents, contracts for, or handles data on behalf of SheperD, Avi/counsel must provide:

1. Certificate of formation/incorporation and exact legal/trade names.
2. Jurisdiction, registration number, registered office, business address, and good standing.
3. EIN/W-9 or equivalent and banking/payee identity.
4. Board, ownership, authorized signatory, and delegation evidence.
5. Signed Michael engagement covering scope, title, hours, compensation, budget, confidentiality, IP, systems, data, geography, termination, and authority.

Until admitted, the website footer is a `company-claim`, not legal-entity verification. Confidence: high. Source: SRC-016.

## Diagnostic integrity

The current Typeform behavior is deterministic, not evidence that AI analyzes documents. Its result does not use port or entered D&D spend, despite the public representation that the estimate is based on volume and primary ports. Evidence status: `mixed`; confidence: high on observed configuration. Source: [[10_Sources/Source - SheperD Diagnostic Typeform - 2026-07-15]].

Required decision:

- Remove the recovery estimate, or disclose and substantiate the exact model, population, period, error bands, exclusions, and reviewer approval.
- Align 24-hour versus 48–72-hour wording with measured operations.
- Publish an accessible privacy notice before relying on public lead intake.
- Never infer document-processing capability from the current form.

- Customer, audit, invoice, dispute, win, loss, no-decision, and recovery counts.
