# Sales Infrastructure and Marketing Assets

## Outcome

Versioned assets that improve discovery, data handoff, and follow-up without inventing proof.

## Initial assets

- Persona talk tracks and discovery checklist.
- Objection library with evidence requirements.
- Secure data-request checklist.
- Opportunity handoff and loss/no-decision checklist.
- Approved proof pack and sample audit when available.
