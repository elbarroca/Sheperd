# FMC 2025 Court Update

## Source facts

- On 2025-09-23, the D.C. Circuit set aside only 46 CFR §541.4, which had limited who could receive a D&D invoice.
- The FMC states that the rest of Part 541 remains effective.
- Current eCFR marks §541.4 as reserved.
- Invoice content and 30-day issuance requirements remain applicable.

## Required correction

Do not repeat the original categorical statement that only a contracting party or consignee may be billed. Current §541.6 still requires the invoice to state the basis for the billed party’s liability.
