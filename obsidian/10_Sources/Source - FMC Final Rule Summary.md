# FMC Final Rule Summary

## Source facts

- FMC published the final Part 541 rule in February 2024.
- Most provisions and later-cleared invoice-content provisions became effective 2024-05-28.
- The rule establishes invoice content, issuance timing, and fee-mitigation/refund/waiver processes.
- Issuance and dispute-resolution timing is generally 30 calendar days as detailed in current Part 541.

## Current-law caution

The original summary describes §541.4’s billing-party limitation. The D.C. Circuit set that section aside in 2025. Use [[10_Sources/Source - 46 CFR Part 541]] and [[10_Sources/Source - FMC 2025 Court Update]] for current status.
