# FTC Advertising Substantiation

## Operational takeaway

Objective marketing claims require a reasonable evidentiary basis before dissemination. SheperD should preserve methodology and support for numerical recovery, invoice-error, automation, timing, and customer-outcome claims before publication.

## Boundary

Legal review must determine the required support for each exact claim and channel. This note is not legal advice.
