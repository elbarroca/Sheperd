# 46 U.S.C. §41301 — Complaints

## Source fact

A person may file a sworn complaint with the FMC alleging a violation of the relevant part. If filed within three years after the claim accrues, the complainant may seek reparations for injury caused by the violation.

The recorded GovInfo resolver exposes the latest published U.S. Code edition (2024 at final QA). The House OLRC preliminary-edition page was checked on 2026-07-15 for current admission but timed out during repeated final link QA; later amendments remain a reviewer check.

## Use limitation

This is a complaint limitation, not a blanket right to refunds for all invoices from the prior 36 months. Accrual, applicability, evidence, liability, violation, injury, route, and remedy remain case-specific.
