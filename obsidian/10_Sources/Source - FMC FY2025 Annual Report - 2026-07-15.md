# FMC FY2025 Annual Report — 2026-07-15

## Source facts

The report records 296 Charge Complaints received, 164 accepted for investigation, $2,893,937.98 refunded, waived, or cancelled during FY2025, and more than $6.1 million cumulative relief since OSRA. Evidence status is `verified`; confidence is high.

## Interpretation boundary

These figures exclude direct carrier disputes, vendor/counsel recoveries, and many MTO disputes. They are not an invalid-charge rate, recoverable-share estimate, or market-size denominator.
