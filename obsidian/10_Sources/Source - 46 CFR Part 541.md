# 46 CFR Part 541 — Current

## Current status checked

The eCFR displayed Title 46 as current through 2026-07-13 on final review.

## Operational points

- Applies to D&D invoices issued by ocean common carriers, MTOs, and NVOCCs; excludes billing relationships among/between carriers and MTOs.
- §541.4 is reserved after the 2025 court decision.
- §541.5: missing required minimum information eliminates the billed party’s obligation to pay the applicable charge.
- §541.6: requires identifying, timing, rate, dispute, and certification information, including the basis for billed-party liability.
- §541.7(a): a billing party generally must issue within 30 calendar days after the charge was last incurred; an untimely charge need not be paid.
- §541.7(b): an NVOCC generally must issue its invoice within 30 calendar days from the **issuance date** of the D&D invoice it received; this is not keyed to receipt date.
- §541.7(c): when an NVOCC is both billed and billing party for the same charge, specified notice of its billed party's dispute triggers an additional 30-day dispute window from the NVOCC's billing party.
- §541.7(d): an invoice corrected to the proper billed party must still be issued within 30 calendar days after the charge was last incurred.
- §541.8: requires at least 30 calendar days for a mitigation/refund/waiver request and an attempt to resolve within 30 days unless extended by agreement.
