# Company Brief

## One-sentence description

SheperD is positioning itself as a contingency-based D&D invoice audit and recovery service for U.S. importers. It says it reviews charges and supports case-specific disputes and recovery through a success-fee model.

## Problem

Importers may pay demurrage and detention charges affected by invoice defects, timing, operational constraints, free-time errors, holds, closures, or other case facts. Federal rules and complaint routes are fact-specific, and some historic billing rules have changed or been vacated. Disputes require invoice review, operational evidence, carrier-specific processes, and deadline discipline that many finance and operations teams may not handle systematically.

## Proposed solution

SheperD says it will:

1. Receive historical and ongoing D&D invoices.
2. Audit invoice and operational facts against FMC requirements and supporting records.
3. Build evidence-backed disputes.
4. Pursue carrier follow-up.
5. Track recovery and charge a success fee only after recovery.

Steps 1–5 describe the marketed offer. The extent of current automation, portal capability, and completed recovery evidence is not publicly verified.

## Likely category

The narrowest supported category is **contingency-based D&D invoice audit and recovery service**. “Fintech,” “regtech,” “AI freight audit,” “Refund As A Service,” “Revenue As a Service,” and “automated recovery” are positioning options, not verified product categories; this language should remain secondary until product maturity is demonstrated.

## Current stage inference

Public evidence points to founder-led commercial validation with a product roadmap under construction:

- The website presents a working automated engine and portal.
- Company-authored technical hiring pages accessible on 2026-07-15 describe building core platform and MVP capability from scratch.
- Public customer proof, case studies, team detail, methodology, and security documentation are absent.

Therefore: treat SheperD as **early validation with unverified product maturity**, not proven SaaS.

## Primary stakeholders

- Economic buyer: CFO, VP Finance, finance leadership.
- Pain owner: supply-chain or logistics leadership.
- Workflow/data owner: operations, AP, logistics analysts.
- Approvers: legal, procurement, IT, security.
- Channel: customs brokers, 3PLs, freight auditors, consultants.
