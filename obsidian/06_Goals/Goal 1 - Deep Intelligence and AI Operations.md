/goal

Build an evidence-controlled, extreme-depth operating intelligence system for SheperD, its founder context, the demurrage and detention industry, Michael's proposed role, every in-scope workflow, and every defensible AI or deterministic-automation opportunity. ## 5. Evidence admission and research protocol

### Source hierarchy

1. Current statutes, regulations, court/FMC materials, official government data, and other primary authoritative sources.
2. Company contracts, demonstrations, system evidence, approved internal data, and signed decisions.
3. Company-controlled website, LinkedIn, forms, job listings, and founder statements as `company-claim` or dated observation.
4. Reputable secondary analysis used only for context or source discovery.
5. Inference, clearly labeled and never promoted silently.

Model sources, claims, and their relationships separately:

- Source: `source_id`, title, authority, author/publisher, URL/path, checked date, publication/effective date, jurisdiction, evidence status, review expiry, and notes.
- Claim: `claim_id`, exact normalized claim, topic, evidence state, confidence, publication status, intended use, conflict group, and canonical note.
- Evidence edge: `evidence_edge_id`, `claim_id`, `source_id`, relationship (`supports`, `contradicts`, `qualifies`, `context-only`), exact passage locator or section, assessment, and checked date.

Primary keys are `source_id`, `claim_id`, and `evidence_edge_id`. The source-claim relationship is many-to-many. No unique-ID check may treat `source_id` as unique inside the evidence-edge dataset.

Rules:

- Prefer two authoritative corroborations for material external claims when feasible.
- Stop after two authoritative corroborations unless a conflict remains.
- After three materially different failed source attempts, mark the answer `unknown` and add a diligence question.
- Company claims prove what the company says, not that the statement is true.
- A search-result snippet is discovery evidence only; admit the underlying source.
- Capture no paywalled, restricted, confidential, or customer material by circumvention.
- Quote minimally. Paraphrase and link.
- Regulatory sources older than 30 days must be refreshed before supporting external wording.
- If sources conflict, preserve both, label the state `mixed`, and identify what evidence would resolve it.

## 9. Time-savings and value model

Use a monthly normalized model for each candidate and low/base/high scenarios. Keep labor time, non-labor spend, one-time implementation, and steady-state economics separate:

```text
eligible_frequency_per_month = min(
  frequency_per_month,
  measured_eligible_cases_per_month
)

modeled_baseline_hours = eligible_frequency_per_month
  * baseline_minutes / 60

eligible_baseline_hours = min(
  modeled_baseline_hours,
  measured_eligible_workload_hours
)

future_operating_hours = eligible_frequency_per_month * (
  assisted_minutes
  + review_minutes
  + exception_rate * exception_minutes
) / 60

gross_hours_released = max(
  0,
  eligible_baseline_hours
  - future_operating_hours
  - monthly_maintenance_labor_hours
)

risk_adjusted_hours = min(
  gross_hours_released
  * adoption_rate
  * technical_availability_rate,
  measured_available_capacity_hours
)

annual_capacity_value = risk_adjusted_hours
  * 12
  * loaded_hourly_cost

implementation_labor_cost = implementation_labor_hours
  * loaded_hourly_cost

year_1_net_value = annual_capacity_value
  - annual_tooling_nonlabor_cost
  - annual_maintenance_nonlabor_cost
  - one_time_setup_nonlabor_cost
  - implementation_labor_cost

steady_state_annual_net_value = annual_capacity_value
  - annual_tooling_nonlabor_cost
  - annual_maintenance_nonlabor_cost

monthly_recurring_net_value = steady_state_annual_net_value / 12

total_one_time_cost = one_time_setup_nonlabor_cost
  + implementation_labor_cost

payback_months = if monthly_recurring_net_value > 0
  then total_one_time_cost / monthly_recurring_net_value
  else "not-achieved"
```

Required fields:

`model_id`, workflow/opportunity IDs, scenario, unit, frequency, measured eligible cases per month, eligible frequency per month, baseline minutes, measured eligible workload hours, measured available capacity hours, assisted minutes, review minutes, exception rate, exception minutes, monthly maintenance labor hours, adoption rate, technical availability rate, loaded cost, annual tooling non-labor cost, annual maintenance non-labor cost, one-time setup non-labor cost, implementation labor hours, gross hours, risk-adjusted hours, annual capacity value, Year-1 net value, steady-state annual net value, monthly recurring net value, total one-time cost, payback months, source/assumption, sample size, confidence, owner, dependency, exclusion/double-count group, and checked date.

Rules:

- `low <= base <= high` for benefits and document inverse risk/cost ordering where relevant.
- Prevent double counting with unique workflow IDs and explicit exclusion/dependency groups.
- `exception_rate` is the share of assisted runs requiring the separately modeled exception rework. `adoption_rate` is the share of eligible cases that actually use the workflow. `technical_availability_rate` covers only tool/service availability, not output quality or exception effort. Do not use overlapping penalties.
- Cap every workflow by measured eligible workload and the portfolio total by Michael's measured capacity for the modeled responsibilities.
- Calculate baseline and future effort over the same `eligible_frequency_per_month`; never compare all cases against only an eligible subset.
- Benefits are **modeled capacity released**, not guaranteed savings, headcount reduction, or revenue.
- High confidence requires instrumented logs or at least five representative observations.
- Medium confidence may use an owner estimate or multiple relevant benchmarks.
- Low confidence means an unvalidated assumption and creates a measurement task.
- Do not assign an hourly cost without a sourced internal decision; show time separately when cost is unknown.
- Show active time and wait time separately. Do not claim wait reduction as labor savings.
- Monthly maintenance labor reduces released hours and must not be subtracted again as money. Only non-labor maintenance spend is subtracted as cost.
- Report Year-1 and steady-state value separately. Do not label a one-time implementation-adjusted result as recurring annual value.
- If monthly recurring net value is zero or negative, payback is `not-achieved`, not infinity or zero.
- Recompute every formula from CSV inputs; do not hand-type chart totals.
- Include sensitivity to adoption, exception rate, review burden, technical availability, volume, and maintenance.
- Rank by impact, effort, evidence confidence, risk, and time to validated learning, not by theoretical automation percentage.
