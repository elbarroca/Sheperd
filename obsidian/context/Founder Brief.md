# Founder Brief

## Answer first

SheperD has a credible problem space and a substantially improved research and GTM operating system. It does not yet have admitted evidence for a validated commercial motion, safe customer-data intake, proven outcomes, or scale readiness.

## Four separate truths

| Dimension | Current | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Research-system maturity | 9.3/10 | Evidence controls and current research are strong |
| GTM system design | 9.2/10 | The operating design is coherent and queryable |
| Real market evidence | 1.8/10 | No authorized comparable customer cohort exists |
| Safe execution readiness | 3.3/10 | Authority, product, claims, security, commercial, and capacity gates remain open |

## What is valuable now

- A founder-queryable evidence and decision system.
- A corrected 16-week learning plan with explicit gates.
- Buyer, partner, content, CRM, measurement, and AI-control designs.
- Twelve visible blockers with owners, evidence requirements, and next actions.
- A controlled experiment register that cannot rank blocked work into execution.

## What is not established

- A proven product, customer result, recovery rate, conversion rate, or market size.
- An approved fee model, recovery waterfall, contract route, or partner economics.
- Secure D2/D3 intake, system ownership, retention, deletion, or incident controls.
- Delivery capacity, reviewer SLA, warm-list availability, or a repeatable acquisition channel.

## Next decision

Run one founder truth-and-gates workshop. Admit or reject GAP-001 through GAP-012, name accountable owners, and record an explicit external-activation `GO` or `NO-GO`.
