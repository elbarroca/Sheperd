# Founder Intelligence Product Context

## Primary users

- Avi and the SheperD founders, who need to understand the current decision posture quickly.
- Michael, who needs an operating map for the commercial system and its approval boundaries.
- Ricardo, whose interpretation must remain visibly separate from source fact and founder decision.

## Core job

In under five minutes, a founder should be able to answer:

1. What can we responsibly decide now?
2. Why is external activation still on hold?
3. Which decision should happen next?
4. Which source and evidence state support each conclusion?

## Product principles

- Lead with posture, reason, and next decision.
- Keep fact, Ricardo analysis, and founder decision separate.
- Observed counts outrank planning scores.
- Eligibility outranks weighted priority.
- Every chart must support a decision and remain understandable as text.
- Progressive disclosure should reveal evidence without hiding the decision.
- No interface control can upgrade evidence or unlock external execution.

## Voice

Calm, direct, plain English, and evidence-first. Avoid hype, slogans, false precision, and unsupported readiness language.

## Anti-patterns

- Oversized all-caps headlines.
- Walls of equal-weight cards.
- Tiny metadata or decorative labels.
- Score bars presented as objective probability.
- Decorative charts that do not change a decision.
- Hidden hold status on small screens.
