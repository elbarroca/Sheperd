# GTM Market and Demand Scan — 2026-07-15

## Decision answer

There is current public information-seeking and vendor activity around D&D invoice requirements, disputes, audit/recovery, pricing, port closures, and evidence. This proves a visible problem/category conversation, not search volume, buyer intent, willingness to pay, or SheperD demand. Exact U.S. keyword volume remains `unknown` because no approved Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner, or equivalent export was admitted.

## Exact public queries checked

- `demurrage detention invoice audit importer`
- `OSRA demurrage invoice requirements dispute`
- `demurrage recovery service pricing`
- `detention fees port closure importer`

The scan used public web search on 2026-07-15. A direct Google Trends extraction attempt did not return usable data. Result order and snippets are volatile and are not a volume measure.

## Current theme map

| Query family | Current visible result types | Buyer question inferred | Safe content use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice requirements | eCFR/FMC explainers, carrier and forwarder dispute pages, audit vendors | “Is the invoice complete and what must it show?” | Source-backed checklist with current-rule review |
| Dispute process | FMC complaint routes, carrier/forwarder instructions, recovery vendors | “Where, when, and with what evidence do we dispute?” | Route comparison that preserves human/legal review |
| Audit/recovery pricing | Success-fee vendors, invoice-audit platforms, free-review offers | “What work is done, how is value realized, and what does it cost?” | Transparent offer mechanics only after commercial approval |
| Port closure and operational evidence | FMC case updates, carrier procedures, practitioner/community discussions | “Which facts matter when cargo or equipment could not move?” | Evidence-readiness education; never blanket eligibility language |
| Prevention versus recovery | Visibility, audit-before-pay, recovery, and broad freight-audit vendors | “Should we prevent, audit, dispute, or outsource?” | Category-alternative comparison without superiority claims |

Community discussions were used only to identify question wording. They are not evidence of prevalence, legal meaning, or product-market fit.

## Authoritative operating signals

- The [FMC VOCC Audit Program](https://www.fmc.gov/databases-and-publications/vessel-operating-common-carrier-vocc-audit-program/) identifies three recurring control areas: accessible rates/terms, clear dispute policies and required information, and clear invoices/cargo-availability notices. This supports audit-trail and evidence-readiness messaging.
- The [Port of Los Angeles 2026 statistics](https://portoflosangeles.org/business/statistics/container-statistics/historical-teu-statistics-2026) show material and concentrated loaded-import activity at one U.S. port. This supports testing port/carrier concentration as an account signal; it does not reveal buyer count, D&D incidence, or recoverability.
- Current FMC rule and complaint sources remain controlled in [[10_Sources/Source - 46 CFR Part 541]] and [[10_Sources/Source - 46 USC 41310 and FMC Complaint Routes - 2026-07-15]].

## Current vendor signals

- [Dockline](https://docklineai.com/) markets broad drayage invoice audit, contract checks, terminal-data verification, and dispute-ready evidence. Performance and maturity are vendor claims.
- [Unwaived](https://www.unwaived.com/) markets recovery-as-a-service, a success fee, finance reporting, and port/carrier-focused pilots. Its displayed pricing and proof remain internally inconsistent or simulated.
- [HarborClaim](https://harborclaim.com/) markets free invoice review, dispute packets, and recovery tracking. Performance remains unverified.
- [Portside Recovery](https://portsiderecovery.com/) shows a specialist success-fee model in Australia. It is an adjacent geographic example, not direct U.S. market proof.

## Buying triggers to test, not assume

- A new or recurring D&D invoice cohort reaches finance/AP.
- A dispute deadline or evidence gap creates urgency.
- Finance cannot reconcile requested versus realized cash, credit, waiver, or cancellation.
- Logistics sees recurring exposure by carrier, terminal, port, or exception type.
- A carrier, contract, portal, or internal-process change creates uncertainty.
- Audit backlog, fragmented records, or approver delay becomes visible.

## Missing evidence

- Query volume, trend, geography, paid-search economics, and SheperD site impressions/clicks.
- Buyer-level D&D incidence, spend distribution, active recovery intent, and willingness to pay.
- Segment response, secure-submission, case quality, delivery effort, outcome, and cycle evidence.

Admit Search Console/Keyword Planner data only with query, geography, period, match type, volume/impression definition, source, and export date. Do not use public result counts as demand volume.
