# Industry, Congestion, and Market Reports — 2026-07-15

## Decision answer

Official sources support a large importer universe, material D&D charges, and measurable disruption/congestion. None supplies the missing bridge from total activity or charges to eligible, disputable, recoverable, and SheperD-obtainable value. TAM, SAM, and SOM therefore remain `unknown`.

## Commercial market reports — discovery only

Published freight-audit-and-payment estimates conflict materially:

- [Knowledge Sourcing Intelligence / Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6176216/freight-audit-payment-market-forecasts) displays about $822M for 2025.
- [The Insight Partners](https://www.theinsightpartners.com/reports/freight-audit-and-payment-market) displays about $994M for 2025.
- Other public market-report pages display multi-billion-dollar values with different scopes.

Evidence status: `unverified-commercial-estimate`; confidence: high that the displayed estimates are non-comparable. Methodology, segment definition, ocean/D&D share, and raw data are not available in the public summaries. These values are excluded from SheperD TAM/SAM/SOM.

## Congestion versus recoverability

Congestion, route disruption, climate events, labor constraints, and customs holds can increase delay and invoice complexity. They do not automatically make a charge unreasonable or recoverable. A valid case still requires the governing terms, current rule, charge dates, availability, holds, appointments, closures, actions by each party, invoice content, dispute timing, evidence, route, and reviewer decision.

## Approved monitoring uses

- Port/trade conditions as account-research context.
- Carrier/port concentration as a cohort stratification variable.
- Disruption date ranges as evidence-retrieval prompts.
- GSCPI/CPPI/PortWatch as macro context with source and date.
- No automatic eligibility, recovery score, or market-size multiplier.
