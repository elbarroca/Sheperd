# FMC D&D Data

## Source fact

The FMC reports that nine major carriers collected roughly **$15.4B** in D&D charges from 2020-04-01 through 2025-03-31. The site also tracks indexed billed, collected, and waived trends.

## Interpretation boundary

This is total collected D&D in the reported carrier population. It does not establish what portion was invalid, unreasonable, disputed, unlawful, or recoverable. It cannot substantiate SheperD’s $2.1B or $6.2B claims without a separate auditable model.
