# FMC FY2025 Charge Complaints

## Source fact

The FMC FY2025 Annual Report records **296 Charge Complaints received**, **164 accepted for investigation**, and **$2,893,937.98 refunded, waived, or cancelled** during FY2025. It reports more than **$6.1M** in cumulative relief since OSRA.

## Interpretation boundary

This is visible Charge Complaint relief, not all direct carrier mitigation, all D&D disputes, a market recovery rate, or total recoverable value. It should be used as evidence that the route produces real relief and that measured public recoveries remain small relative to total D&D collections.
