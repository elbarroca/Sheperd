# World Shipping Council Part 541 Opinion — 2026-07-15

## Source fact

The D.C. Circuit set aside only 46 CFR §541.4 after finding the FMC had not reconciled its contractual-privity rationale with the rule's categorical exclusion. The remainder of Part 541 was severed and remained operative. Evidence status is `verified`; confidence is high.

## Boundary

The opinion does not itself determine proper-party liability for any SheperD case. Current §541.6 still requires the invoice to state why the billed party is liable.
