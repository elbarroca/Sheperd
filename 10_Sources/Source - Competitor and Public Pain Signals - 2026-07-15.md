# Competitor and Public Pain Signals — 2026-07-15

## Decision answer

SheperD operates inside a congested stack: specialist D&D recovery, drayage/invoice audit, enterprise freight audit/payment, internal teams, brokers, consultants, and counsel. Official cases demonstrate real billing and liability disputes. Vendor and sponsored content demonstrates category positioning, not independent outcomes.

## Competitive implications to test

1. **Narrow wedge:** explain why a customer should choose SheperD over broad FAP, a direct specialist, or an existing broker/counsel relationship.
2. **Evidence UX:** prove faster evidence completeness, deadline visibility, and decision traceability—not generic AI.
3. **Negative decisions:** measure value from stopping weak cases before effort or relationship cost grows.
4. **Data boundary:** demonstrate secure handling, provenance, reviewer ownership, and deletion/retention controls.
5. **Outcome proof:** compare realized cash/credit/waiver, customer work, cycle, quality, and net fee on like-for-like cases.
6. **Commercial truth:** record fee base, exclusions, minimums, caps, attribution, and treatment of prevented versus recovered value.
