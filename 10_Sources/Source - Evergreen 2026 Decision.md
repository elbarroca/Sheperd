# Evergreen 2026 Decision

## Source facts

The D.C. Circuit opinion is dated 2026-04-28. On 2026-07-08, the FMC reported that the court upheld its determination that detention fees during a three-day port closure were unreasonable where the trucker had no practical ability to return the equipment earlier. The court endorsed the FMC’s fact-specific use of the freight-fluidity incentive principle.

## Use limitation

This supports evidence-led review of impossible-return periods; it is not a blanket rule that every closure, weekend, or disruption invalidates a charge.
