# FMC Charge Complaint Guidance

## Source facts

- Any person may submit information about potentially non-compliant common-carrier charges under 46 U.S.C. §41310.
- Supporting documents can include invoices, bills of lading, payment evidence, appointment screenshots, gate closures, and communications.
- For D&D charges, the common carrier bears the burden of establishing reasonableness in the charge-complaint process.
- A carrier may voluntarily refund or waive a charge.
- FMC investigation is enforcement activity, not representation of the complainant or a refund guarantee.

## Product caution

The process remains interim and may change. Do not hard-code the present submission method into durable product claims.
