# Competitor Websites — 2026-07-15

## Observed positioning

- [BlueCargo](https://www.bluecargo.io/post/are-you-ready-to-navigate-the-final-fmc-ruling): OSRA/invoice checking, event/contract validation, disputes, and historical/pre-payment audit.
- [Unwaived](https://www.unwaived.com/): D&D recovery-as-a-service, evidence packets, filing, credit tracking, and published 25–35% success-fee range.
- [HarborClaim](https://harborclaim.com/): invoice review, evidence-backed dispute packets, and recovery tracking.
- [AuditDray](https://auditdray.com/): AI-positioned invoice audit and dispute generation.
- [MiraLedger](https://www.miraledger.io/): carrier-invoice audit, dispute packs, and recovery reporting.
- [Intelligent Audit](https://www.intelligentaudit.com/case-studies/reducing-demurrage-and-detention-costs-through-invoice-validation): broader freight audit with a vendor-reported D&D case study.
- [GoComet](https://www.gocomet.com/solutions/reduce-supply-chain-costs): prevention/visibility alternative.
