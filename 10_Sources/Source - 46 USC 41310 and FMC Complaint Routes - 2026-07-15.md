# 46 U.S.C. §41310 and FMC Complaint Routes — 2026-07-15

## Charge Complaint

Any person may submit information about a potentially non-compliant common-carrier charge. Bills of lading and invoices are required, and the common carrier bears the burden on D&D reasonableness in this process. Evidence status is `verified`; confidence is high.

## Route distinctions

- The [interim Charge Complaint procedure](https://www.fmc.gov/ocean-shipping-reform-act-of-2022-implementation/guidance-on-charge-complaint-interim-procedure/) is enforcement intake, not representation or a refund guarantee.
- It is distinct from formal complaints, small claims, and [CADRS](https://www.fmc.gov/complaints-and-assistance/).
- The current guidance generally excludes pre-2022-06-16 charges, MTO-only charges not assessed on behalf of a carrier, charges not yet invoiced, transactions outside the described U.S. import/export nexus, and non-charge conduct.
- An unsuccessful Charge Complaint does not itself bar a later formal or small-claim filing.

## Unknown

No permanent final Charge Complaint procedure was located after the interim guidance. Current mechanics require refresh before product hard-coding or external instruction.
