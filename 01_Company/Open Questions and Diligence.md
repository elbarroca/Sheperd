# Open Questions and Diligence

## P0 — answer before external activation

- [ ] What is the registered legal entity, jurisdiction, address, and authority to contract?
- [ ] Who are all founders, team members, and accountable owners?
- [ ] Is Michael’s engagement signed, including compensation, confidentiality, IP, authority, and termination?
- [ ] What works today: manual service, MVP, production engine, and portal?
- [ ] What evidence supports every claim in [[01_Company/Claims and Evidence Register]]?
- [ ] Which recovery route is used: direct carrier dispute, FMC charge complaint, formal/small claim, ADR, or counsel?
- [ ] Who provides regulatory/legal review and within what SLA?
- [ ] How are invoices and operational records transferred, secured, retained, and deleted?
- [ ] What are the fee, “recovered” definition, payment mechanics, and partner economics?
- [ ] What is the cap table, funding history, current runway, and authority for future fundraising statements?
- [ ] Who owns the brand, domain, source code, models, datasets, checks, and customer-created work product?
- [ ] Which APIs, websites, tariffs, and carrier data may be accessed, copied, or automated under license and terms?
- [ ] What professional, cyber, general-liability, and directors-and-officers insurance is active?
- [ ] What founder and key-person reference, conflict, litigation, and sanctions checks are required for the engagement?

## P1 — answer before scaling outreach

- [ ] How many real customers, audits, invoices, disputes, wins, losses, and dollars recovered exist?
- [ ] Which segments, carriers, ports, and invoice patterns have actual evidence?
- [ ] What is the turnaround capacity from data submission to validated result?
- [ ] Which data fields are mandatory, and who follows up on incomplete data?
- [ ] What can be shown in an anonymized case study with permission?
- [ ] What product and service claims are roadmap-only?
- [ ] What CRM, analytics, secure storage, and case tools are approved?
- [ ] Which subprocessors and international transfers are involved between U.S. customers, Israel-based personnel, and other jurisdictions?

## P2 — answer before repeatable scale

- [ ] What is the first-hire profile and capacity model?
- [ ] Which partner type produces accepted, qualified referrals?
- [ ] What are gross-margin and cash-cycle economics by archetype?
- [ ] What is the canonical category and brand language?
- [ ] Which controls are required for enterprise security review?

## Evidence required

Prefer contracts, product demonstrations, system screenshots with sensitive data removed, anonymized case records, approved process maps, CRM exports, and primary regulatory sources. Founder recollection alone is a hypothesis.
