## Approval workflow

1. Add the exact proposed sentence and intended channel.
2. Link primary evidence and methodology.
3. Domain/legal reviewer checks legal meaning.
4. Avi approves commercial use.
5. Record approval date, expiry/review date, and constraints.

Draft assets must show their claim IDs sentence by sentence. `verified context` is not automatic publication approval; the exact wording, channel, date, and reviewer still govern.
