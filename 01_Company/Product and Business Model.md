# Product and Business Model

## Marketed workflow

`Invoice intake → compliance and operational checks → evidence file → dispute and follow-up → claimed customer recovery → SheperD success fee`

## Capability truth table

| Capability | Public presentation | Evidence state | Required confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice intake | Historical plus ongoing invoices | Company claim | Accepted formats, secure channel, volume limits |
| 160+ checks | Automated engine | Unverified | Check catalog, test evidence, manual versus automated split |
| Evidence building | Engine prepares an evidence file | Company claim | Inputs, reviewer, confidence and exception handling |
| Dispute filing | SheperD handles filing and follow-up | Company claim | Carrier channels, authority, reviewer, SLAs |
| Portal | Tracks invoices, claims, and recoveries | Mixed/unresolved | Live demo, current users, roadmap distinction |
| Recovery flow | Carrier pays customer directly | Company claim | Cash versus credit, proof, reconciliation process |
| Recovery estimate | Website says 24 hours | Mixed | Typeform says 48–72 hours; define actual SLA |

## Commercial model

Publicly stated:

- No upfront audit fee.
- Success fee or revenue share only after recovery.

Unknown:

- Fee percentage and minimum economics.
- Definition of “recovered” when value is a carrier credit.
- Payment timing, clawbacks, tax, disputes, and contract term.
- Whether prevention or ongoing monitoring has separate pricing.
- Partner referral economics and account ownership.
- Whether pricing varies by segment or workflow.
- Whether any subscription, integration, commitment, exclusivity, or minimum term applies.

## Recommended product-state vocabulary

- **Current service:** what SheperD can deliver today with humans and existing tools.
- **Current MVP:** capabilities demonstrated end to end on real, permitted data.
- **Pilot capability:** bounded feature available only with operational support.
- **Roadmap:** not yet available; never use present tense externally.

## Required founder workshop

Answer with demonstrations or artifacts, not adjectives:

1. What can a customer use today?
2. What happens manually behind the scenes?
3. Which checks exist, and how many are deterministic?
4. What recovery routes have been used?
5. How many invoices, disputes, customers, and recoveries have been completed?
6. What data is collected, where is it stored, and who can access it?
7. What constitutes success-fee eligibility?
