- Region / lane: us / regulatory

#### Three key points
- Four enforcement areas cover near-shore waters in South Florida, Central/Southern California, Puerto Rico, and the Gulf Coast of Texas.
- CBP states the areas are intended to support interdiction of vessels used for smuggling illegal cargoes.
