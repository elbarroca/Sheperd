What each piece does

Tavily is the web research layer: it discovers candidate pages and extracts direct source content.
LangGraph is the workflow layer: it routes state, budgets, checkpoints, agents, and stop gates.
Neon PostgreSQL is the canonical evidence store; database cost is $0 for now. No blockchain is used in the current design.
