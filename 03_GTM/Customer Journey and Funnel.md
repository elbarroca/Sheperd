# Customer Journey and Funnel

## Critical insight

The likely bottleneck is not initial interest; it is moving a prospect from meeting to a **secure, complete, valid data submission** that the product/data team can evaluate on time.

## Journey

1. Awareness or partner referral.
2. Credibility check: company, product, claims, proof, security.
3. Discovery and qualification.
4. Data request accepted.
5. Data received securely and marked pending validation.
6. Submission accepted as valid, returned incomplete, or rejected with a reason.
7. Analysis and potential value confirmed.
8. Commercial/legal proposal.
9. Recovery engagement starts.
10. Dispute outcome and customer value realized.
11. Case study, expansion, or referral with permission.

## CRM stage contract

| Stage | Entry evidence | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Account sourced with provenance | Contactable account and rationale |
| Contactable | Valid account/contact route | First reviewed touch logged |
| Contacted | Activity recorded | Response or closed/no-response outcome |
| Engaged | Positive two-way response | Meeting held or explicit decline |
| Qualified meeting held | Required discovery fields captured | Pain, owner, evidence, urgency, next step pass criteria |
| Qualified opportunity | Fit and path to value exist | Data request accepted |
| Data request accepted | Owner and secure method agreed | Required package received into approved storage |
| Submission pending validation | Receipt, case ID, and checklist version recorded | Accepted, incomplete, or rejected status assigned |
| Submission incomplete | Missing items, owner, and due date recorded | Package resubmitted or closed with reason |
| Submission rejected | Product/data owner records a controlled reason | Rework explicitly authorized or opportunity closed |
| Data validated | Product/data owner marks completeness | Analysis result and confidence recorded |
| Value confirmed | Credible opportunity quantified | Proposal delivered |
| Proposal | Scope, economics, authority defined | Procurement/legal or closed outcome |
| Closed won/lost/no decision | Final outcome and reason recorded | Handoff or learning completed |

## Data-submission checklist — metadata only

- NDA/DPA and authority status.
- Secure storage location link.
- Invoice period and count.
- Carrier, port, and shipment coverage.
- Required contract/tariff and operational evidence status.
- Missing items and owner.
- Date requested, received, validated, and rejected/accepted.
- Product/data reviewer and SLA.

Do not store the underlying files in Obsidian.

Use [[04_Operations/Customer Data Intake and Security]] as the controlling intake policy.

## Bottleneck questions

- What security or authority concern stops submission?
- Which documents are hardest to locate?
- Does the customer understand what happens after upload?
- Can the product/data team respond within the promised time?
- Is estimated value credible enough to justify effort?
- Which stakeholder must approve release?
