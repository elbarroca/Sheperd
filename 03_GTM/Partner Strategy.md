# Partner Strategy

## Priority partner types

1. Customs brokers and freight forwarders with trusted importer relationships.
2. 3PLs and supply-chain consultants.
3. Freight audit/pay providers that lack specialized D&D recovery.
4. Port, carrier, trade, and finance advisors with qualified access.
5. Legal/regulatory specialists for bounded review—not lead resale.

## Channel hypotheses

| Channel | Primary hypothesis | Required evidence | Primary risk | Activation state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Founder-led warm | Trusted introductions may improve held-meeting quality | Permissioned introduction cohort, message version, held/qualified/submission outcomes | Founder dependency and selection bias | Blocked |
| Customs broker / forwarder | Repeated client D&D questions may support referrals | Consent, account ownership, accepted-referral quality, delivery SLA | Client trust and channel conflict | Blocked |
| 3PL / consultant | Operational context may improve evidence readiness | Comparable referral cohort, missing-item cycles, outcomes | Data rights and unclear responsibility | Blocked |
| Freight audit/pay | Existing invoice access may support a partner/embed motion | Vendor/customer permission, integration rights, economics, case quality | Competitive overlap and D3 exposure | Blocked |
| Inbound education | Source-backed guidance may produce qualified questions | Search/analytics baseline, consent, query/asset/CTA attribution | Unsupported claims and low-intent traffic | Publishing blocked |
| Direct cold | Narrow, permission-safe account hypotheses may create learning | Admitted source/permission, DNC, separate cohort, reviewed message | Compliance, low trust, cohort contamination | Blocked; lower priority |

Do not compare blended channels. Warm, cold, partner, and inbound require separate cohorts, denominators, wait time, loss reasons, and delivery-quality outcomes.

## Qualification

Evaluate:

- Trusted access to the intended ICP.
- Frequency and quality of relevant referrals.
- Clear client consent and account ownership.
- No channel conflict or misleading representation.
- Willingness to follow secure data and claims rules.
- Economics that preserve delivery quality and gross margin.

## Referral SLA — proposed

- SheperD acknowledges within one business day.
- Michael accepts/rejects fit within two business days.
- Referred contact receives a human-reviewed response within the agreed window.
- Partner receives permitted stage updates without customer PII leakage.
- Attribution, commercial terms, and account ownership are recorded before outreach.
- Product/data capacity and secure-intake availability are checked before accepting a referral.
- The partner receives only the approved minimum stage signal; no customer case details without written permission.

## Partner scorecard

- Referrals received.
- Accepted referral rate.
- Qualified-meeting rate.
- Valid data-submission rate.
- Qualified opportunity and win rate.
- Median cycle time.
- Customer quality issues.
- Revenue and gross margin by partner.

Do not label a partner high-quality from introduction volume alone.

## Stop rules

- No written permission, account ownership, approved claims, referral terms, or secure path: reject/defer the referral.
- Product/data or reviewer SLA fails: stop accepting new referrals.
- Two cohorts are not comparable: record directional learning only.
- A partner requests lead resale, unapproved customer disclosure, legal representation, or guaranteed outcomes: stop and escalate.
