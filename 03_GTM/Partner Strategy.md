# Partner Strategy

## Priority partner types

1. Customs brokers and freight forwarders with trusted importer relationships.
2. 3PLs and supply-chain consultants.
3. Freight audit/pay providers that lack specialized D&D recovery.
4. Port, carrier, trade, and finance advisors with qualified access.
5. Legal/regulatory specialists for bounded review—not lead resale.

## Qualification

Evaluate:

- Trusted access to the intended ICP.
- Frequency and quality of relevant referrals.
- Clear client consent and account ownership.
- No channel conflict or misleading representation.
- Willingness to follow secure data and claims rules.
- Economics that preserve delivery quality and gross margin.

## Referral SLA — proposed

- SheperD acknowledges within one business day.
- Michael accepts/rejects fit within two business days.
- Referred contact receives a human-reviewed response within the agreed window.
- Partner receives permitted stage updates without customer PII leakage.
- Attribution, commercial terms, and account ownership are recorded before outreach.

## Partner scorecard

- Referrals received.
- Accepted referral rate.
- Qualified-meeting rate.
- Valid data-submission rate.
- Qualified opportunity and win rate.
- Median cycle time.
- Customer quality issues.
- Revenue and gross margin by partner.

Do not label a partner high-quality from introduction volume alone.
