# Website and Content Audit

## Outcome

The public surfaces communicate a sharp recovery promise, but unresolved product-state tension, uncited claims, missing trust/security material, and a blank privacy page make aggressive lead generation premature. Findings combine the website, founder-controlled LinkedIn surfaces, and a linked diagnostic Typeform checked on 2026-07-15.

## P0 — trust and truth

- Reconcile current service, MVP, portal, and roadmap language.
- Remove or substantiate every claim in [[01_Company/Claims and Evidence Register]].
- Replace “three years of refunds” with reviewed limitation-language.
- Publish a complete privacy policy and data-intake explanation.
- Add legal entity, team/founder, contact consistency, and basic terms.
- State exactly what files are requested, how they are protected, and what happens next.
- Align 24-hour website promise with the Typeform’s 48–72-hour promise.

## P1 — conversion

- Add a plain-language “What we review / what we need / what you receive” section.
- Show a reviewed, anonymized sample audit or evidence pack.
- Explain fee mechanics, carrier credit versus cash, and no-recovery treatment.
- Add FAQ for eligibility, deadlines, rejection, reissued invoices, security, and workload.
- Use proof only with customer permission and methodology.
- Route each persona to the same primary CTA with appropriate supporting detail.

## P2 — demand generation

- Publish source-backed regulatory explainers.
- Turn approved customer learning into de-identified objection and FAQ content.
- Build CFO, supply-chain, and operations content pillars.
- Use CTA/UTM attribution and measure qualified inquiries, not impressions.
- Pursue podcasts/speaking only after the company truth and proof pack are stable.

## Observed defects

- SEO title/description reportedly remain placeholder design copy.
- Privacy page exposes no usable body content to extract.
- LinkedIn’s website field reportedly points to Typeform rather than sheperd.io.
- Footer email display and link differ.
- Copy includes “IntroducingAn,” “invoce,” “on going,” and an omitted subject in one sentence.
- Typeform estimate reportedly uses container volume × $75 while ignoring port and stated D&D spend; the methodology is not disclosed.

## Content rule

Do not publish case studies, recovery numbers, error rates, customer claims, or “AI” capabilities until the supporting evidence and permissions are recorded.
