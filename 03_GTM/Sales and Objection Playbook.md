# Sales and Objection Playbook

> [!warning] Draft boundary
> Every external-facing block below is `DRAFT - HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`. Claim IDs indicate traceability, not approval. No send is authorized.

## Positioning decision

- Supported category: **contingency-based D&D invoice audit and recovery service**. Claim basis: C-003/C-024/C-026.
- Target buyer hypothesis: U.S. importers/BCOs with a finance owner, recurring D&D exposure, accessible evidence, and manageable carrier/port concentration. Evidence state: `inference`.
- Core problem: invoice, timing, rate, event, and dispute evidence is fragmented across finance and operations while deadlines and review capacity are constrained. Claim basis: C-023/C-025 plus buyer hypothesis.
- Differentiation to prove: better audit trail, lower submission friction, faster reviewed cycles, secure intake, and realized-value reconciliation. None is approved as a superiority claim.
- Service/software boundary: describe the current service only after demonstration; describe software, engine, portal, automation, or AI only at the demonstrated layer. Claim basis: C-005/C-006/C-015/C-021.

## Discovery questions

### Finance

- How much D&D is paid, credited, waived, or written off by period?
- Who owns recovery and approves outside success fees?
- How do carrier credits reach the ledger?
- What proof and security review are required?

### Supply chain and operations

- Which carriers, ports, lanes, and events create repeat exposure?
- How are free time, appointments, holds, closures, pickup, and returns recorded?
- Where do disputes stall?
- What would a useful case timeline contain?

### Data readiness

- Which invoices, contracts/tariffs, BOLs, and event records are available?
- Who can authorize secure transfer?
- What retention and access restrictions apply?
- Who can answer missing-evidence questions?

## Initial objection library

| Objection | What it may mean | Safe response direction | Proof needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| “We already dispute charges.” | Existing process or defensiveness | Ask about coverage, cycle time, backlog, outcome tracking | Comparative audit, not generic superiority claim |
| “Carriers will not pay.” | Past losses or relationship concern | Acknowledge case dependence; explore evidence and process | Qualified examples and route-specific outcomes |
| “We cannot share invoices.” | Security, authority, or workload | Explain approved intake only after controls; offer metadata-first scoping | Privacy/security pack |
| “No budget.” | Fee concern or low urgency | Clarify success-fee concept without quoting unknown terms | Approved fee and recovery definition |
| “We do not have the data.” | Fragmented systems | Identify minimum viable package and owners | Proven checklist and data SLA |
| “OSRA gives us three years anyway.” | Legal oversimplification | Separate complaint limitation from eligibility and carrier deadlines | [[02_Domain/D&D and OSRA Primer]] plus reviewer |
| “How much will we recover?” | Need for business case | Do not invent; provide a bounded audit process and confidence | Approved methodology and real evidence |
| “Is this software or a service?” | Product-maturity and implementation concern | Separate demonstrated current service, MVP, pilot, and roadmap | Product truth table and live permitted demo |
| “Who decides whether a charge is valid?” | Legal/authority concern | Explain deterministic checks versus named human review; do not answer case eligibility | Reviewer identity, route, and approval policy |
| “What happens to our data?” | Security/procurement concern | Stop at the approved data map; no ad-hoc email transfer | Privacy notice, DPA, system/vendor/access/retention evidence |
| “Will this damage carrier relationships?” | Operational and trust concern | Describe factual evidence and human-authorized action; avoid outcome claims | Approved escalation model and customer-controlled authority |

## Qualification rubric

Human-only decision. A qualified opportunity requires all of:

- ICP rationale and dated problem evidence.
- Named economic, operational, and data owners.
- Case-specific path to value without a recovery promise.
- Permitted source and message/cohort version.
- Approved secure-data path and customer authority.
- Product/data reviewer plus available SLA.
- Next action, owner, date, blocker, and disqualifiers recorded.

Any missing mandatory item keeps the record at research/engaged status; no automated qualification or stage change.
