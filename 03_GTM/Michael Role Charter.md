# Michael Role Charter

## Recommended title

**GTM & Revenue Operations Lead**

“Operations and marketing” is too vague for the actual mandate. The role combines commercial discovery, account/partner activation, CRM discipline, founder-knowledge capture, content requirements, and operating cadence.

## Mandate

Turn Avi’s founder knowledge and early market signals into a repeatable, evidence-backed commercial system: qualified conversations, secure data submissions, visible funnel performance, documented learning, and clear decisions about what to continue, change, or stop.

## Owns

- Account and partner segmentation.
- Controlled outreach and discovery execution.
- CRM architecture, hygiene, experiment tags, and reporting.
- Discovery, follow-up, objection capture, and playbook maintenance.
- Website/content requirements and coordination.
- Weekly commercial operating cadence.

## Does not independently own

- Regulatory interpretation or claim eligibility.
- Product-analysis conclusions or customer recovery estimates.
- Pricing, contract, or business-model approval.
- Customer-data acceptance, security, or compliance decisions.
- Website engineering, all content production, hiring, or funding.

## Required support

| Owner | Required contribution |
|---|---|
| Avi | Commercial approval, claims, pricing boundaries, introductions, 24–48-hour decisions |
| Domain reviewer | Regulatory and claims review |
| Product/data owner | Intake validation, analysis, outcome SLA |
| Legal/privacy owner | Engagement, contracts, data controls, publication boundaries |
| Website owner | Implement approved conversion and trust changes |

## 16-week success definition

A measured, reproducible GTM motion with:

- Reliable CRM data and stage definitions.
- Directional evidence about segments, personas, messages, and partners.
- A secure, measurable path from interest to valid data submission.
- Versioned playbooks and decisions.
- Clear unresolved risks and a next 90-day plan.

This does not by itself prove the full commercial model or make Michael a regulatory expert.

## Missing terms to resolve

- Title, hours, location, compensation, budget, decision rights, systems access, customer-data authority, geographic scope, and termination.
- Definition of a deal, qualified opportunity, valid submission, and accepted recovery.
- Founder/product/domain response SLAs.
