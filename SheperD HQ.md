# SheperD HQ

> [!abstract] Current answer
> **SheperD** is positioning itself as a contingency-based demurrage and detention (D&D) audit and recovery company for U.S. importers. It claims to identify non-compliant charges, prepare and pursue disputes, and earn only when money is recovered. The need and regulatory opportunity are real; product maturity, customer proof, numerical marketing claims, recovery mechanics, and data-security controls remain unverified.

## Naming decision

- Canonical company spelling: **SheperD**.
- Canonical founder spelling: **Avi Manaim**, Co-Founder & CEO.
- “Shepherdy,” “Shepherd,” “Avi Namin,” and “Javi” are transcription or narrative variants until Avi confirms otherwise.
- The identity of the other implied co-founder and the legal entity behind “SheperD.IO, Inc.” remain open.

## Four blockers before external activation

1. **Written engagement gate:** authority, compensation, confidentiality, IP, systems access, and data handling must be agreed before external representation or customer-data access.
2. **Product truth gate:** document what is manual today, what the MVP performs, and what remains roadmap.
3. **Claims gate:** substantiate or remove every quantitative and legal marketing claim in [[01_Company/Claims and Evidence Register]].
4. **Data-security gate:** define secure intake, access, retention, deletion, NDA/DPA, and product/data review SLAs.
