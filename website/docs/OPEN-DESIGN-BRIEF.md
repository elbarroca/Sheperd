# Open Design Brief

Status: controlling brief for three clean-room directions

## Assignment

Create three materially different, mobile-first single-page directions for the SheperD no-collection Preview. Use the approved core message in `COPY-DECK.md`; visual exploration may not strengthen, reinterpret, or invent claims.

The page's job is to help importer finance and logistics teams turn an isolated D&D invoice into a generic record checklist, then stop at a qualified human-review boundary.

## Governing idea

**The record before the claim.**

Make evidence alignment visible. The visitor should feel a continuous movement from billing record, to operational record, to governing record, then a deliberate stop.

## Required content

- Audience visible in the first viewport.
- One hero thesis and one primary CTA.
- Three evidence categories with distinct compositions.
- A sequential record-building path.
- A concrete checklist.
- A visible limits/no-collection boundary.
- Current official-source register.
- A finished closing action and footer.

## Three direction territories

### Direction A: Evidence Tide

A continuous route line moves through three spatial layers like a charted passage. Large type behaves like navigational notation; records sit on the route rather than inside cards. Cool deep-water neutrals, oxidized signal orange, and off-white paper. The memorable element is one line whose meaning changes from invoice edge, to event timeline, to source underline.

### Direction B: Margin Notes

The page behaves like a rigorously annotated case file. Wide editorial margins hold source dates, boundary notes, and gaps while the main column carries the checklist. Cobalt pencil marks and cropped macro paper textures create material depth without implying customer documents. The memorable element is a moving margin marker that stops at unresolved evidence.

### Direction C: Terminal Ledger

A precise industrial ledger borrows rhythm from cargo manifests and port signage without showing fabricated product UI. Bold condensed display type, high-contrast ruled fields, and a modular vertical sequence create urgency. The memorable element is a three-part registration mark that aligns only when all record categories are present.

These are territories, not fixed palettes. Open Design must make each materially different in information rhythm, type, crop, image behavior, and motion.

## Original imagery

Use only generated abstract material studies, macro documentary-style still lifes with no people, or code-native graphics. Do not show customers, ports in operation, company offices, legal seals, carrier brands, invoices with data, dashboards, certifications, results, or implied case proof.

Every asset needs prompt, generation method, date, file path, and rights/provenance note.

## Motion

Choose one orchestrated motion idea per direction:

- A: route line draws once as sections enter.
- B: margin marker advances to the current evidence layer.
- C: registration marks align as the checklist sequence completes.

Motion must explain sequence or state, animate only transform/opacity/stroke where practical, and collapse to an equivalent static state under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility and mobile

- Start at 320px and 375px; desktop is an expansion, not the source layout.
- Keep the audience, thesis, and CTA visible without horizontal scroll.
- Semantic HTML, one H1, landmarks, logical headings, visible focus, keyboard path, 44px targets, AA contrast, forced-colors resilience, and 200% reflow.
- No meaning depends on motion, color, crop, hover, or pointer precision.

## Reject

- Generic SaaS hero plus three cards plus logo wall
- Fake dashboard, fabricated product UI, or upload flow
- Gradient text, purple AI glow, glass panels, decorative grids, pill excess, or bento templates
- Customer, certification, legal, or outcome implication
- Repeated card sections
- Desktop stacking presented as mobile design
- Unapproved company, product, legal, commercial, or result claims

## Selection rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Five-second message clarity | 25 |
| Audience relevance | 15 |
| Memorability and visual distinctiveness | 15 |
| Trust and evidence safety | 15 |
| CTA clarity and conversion path | 10 |
| Mobile UX | 10 |
| Accessibility readiness | 5 |
| Performance feasibility | 5 |

No direction wins with a trust failure. Winner threshold: `80/100`.

## Output contract per direction

- Named idea and rationale
- `DESIGN.md` using the active Open Design schema
- Real single-page HTML/CSS artifact
- Asset directory and provenance entry
- 375px and 1440px screenshots
- Motion and reduced-motion plan
- Self-critique against the rubric
