## Section: One charge, three records

**Lead**

The page organizes what to gather. It does not decide what the records mean for a specific case.

### Billing record

Start with the invoice, bill of lading, payment state, charge dates, and the party identified on the bill.

### Operational record

Bring together availability, pickup, return, terminal timing, appointments, holds, closures, and communications.

### Governing record

Locate the free-time terms, tariff or contract language, applicable rule version, route, and relevant dates.

## Section: The sequence

**Heading**

Build the record before framing the question.

1. Gather the billing record.
2. Reconstruct the operational timeline.
3. Match the governing terms and source dates.
4. Mark what is missing.
5. Take the packet to a qualified human reviewer.

## Section: Requirements checklist

**Heading**

What to bring into one review packet

- Invoice and bill of lading
- Payment record
- Free-time, tariff, or contract terms
- Availability, pickup, return, and terminal timing
- Appointment, hold, closure, and communication records

**Empty-state copy**

Missing a category? Mark the gap. Do not fill it with an assumption.
