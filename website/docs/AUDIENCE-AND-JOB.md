# Audience and Job

Status: Preview strategy
Evidence state: audience hypothesis; copy must not imply validation

## Primary audience

Finance, logistics, supply-chain, AP, and operations people at U.S. importers who need to make sense of a demurrage or detention invoice and gather the record for qualified human review.

This is the current internal ICP hypothesis. It has sample size zero and must not be described as a proven segment, customer base, or product adoption signal.

## Secondary stakeholders

- Finance leaders who need an auditable explanation of the charge and next step.
- Logistics and operations owners who hold the event record.
- Legal, procurement, and review stakeholders who decide the appropriate route.
- Brokers, 3PLs, and advisers who may help assemble records without owning the final conclusion.

## Triggering situation

A D&D invoice has arrived, is being reconciled, or needs review. The billing record alone may not explain what happened operationally, which terms governed the event, or which review route is appropriate.

The public Preview does not determine eligibility, compliance, liability, or recovery. It frames the record needed before those decisions.

## Job to be done

> Help me see which billing, operational, and governing records belong in one review packet, so I can identify gaps and take a better-prepared next step with a qualified human reviewer.

## Current alternative and failure mode

The likely current workflow is fragmented across invoices, email, spreadsheets, terminal screenshots, carrier portals, tariff or contract terms, and individual memory. The failure mode is starting with a verdict or deadline assumption before the underlying record has been reconstructed.

This is a workflow hypothesis, not measured customer research.

## Questions the page must answer in five seconds

1. **Who:** importer finance and logistics teams.
2. **Problem:** the invoice is only one part of the review record.
3. **Distinct approach:** assemble the record before framing a conclusion.
4. **Action:** see what to gather.

## Objections and safe answers

| Objection | Evidence-safe response |
|---|---|
| Is this legal advice? | No. The Preview is educational and does not decide a case. |
| Can I upload an invoice? | No. The Preview collects no files or personal information. |
| Does a three-year period make old invoices refundable? | The site does not make that claim; it links to the official statute and suppresses unapproved interpretation. |
| Does SheperD perform this work today? | Current product and service capabilities are unverified and intentionally absent. |
| Why trust the page? | It separates neutral workflow guidance from official sources, dates the source register, and states what it cannot decide. |

## Single visitor job

Move from an isolated invoice to a clear, generic checklist of records worth gathering. The page's primary action is `See what to gather`. The only optional lower-commitment action is `Open official sources`.
