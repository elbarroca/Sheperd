# Positioning

Status: evidence-safe Preview positioning; not a company or product claim

## Positioning statement

For importer finance and logistics teams reviewing a D&D invoice, the SheperD Preview is a source-linked record map that organizes billing facts, operational events, and governing terms before a qualified human review. Unlike a recovery calculator, intake form, or automated verdict, it collects nothing and refuses to imply an outcome.

This statement defines the Preview experience. It must not be reused as a claim about a live SheperD service or product.

## Narrow value proposition

**Turn one invoice into a record checklist.**

The page earns this value by showing three evidence layers and their relationship. It does not promise analysis, filing, recovery, or automation.

## Memorable idea

**The record before the claim.**

The visual system should make evidence alignment tangible: one continuous route moves from bill, to event record, to governing terms, then stops at a clearly marked human-review boundary.

## Message hierarchy

1. Audience: `For importer finance and logistics teams`
2. Thesis: `Before you assess the charge, reconstruct the record.`
3. Explanation: `A practical map of the billing, operational, and governing records that shape a D&D invoice review.`
4. Primary action: `See what to gather`
5. Boundary: `Educational Preview. No uploads. No case decision.`

## Conversion path

```text
Audience recognition
  -> record gap becomes visible
  -> three evidence layers become scannable
  -> generic checklist becomes actionable
  -> official sources and human-review boundary close the page
```

## Credibility without unsupported proof

- Direct links to current primary government sources.
- Source titles, issuers, and checked dates displayed together.
- No customer, result, market-size, product, commercial, or legal claims.
- A visible no-collection and no-decision boundary.
- Production remains fail-closed.

## Voice

Calm, precise, plain, and operational. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No sales theatre.

Use: record, invoice, event, term, date, source, route, review, gather, compare, check.

Avoid: revolutionary, seamless, powerful, intelligent, transform, unlock, optimize, compliant, invalid, eligible, recoverable, unlawful, guaranteed, automated, AI-powered, platform, portal, free audit, success fee.

## One deliberate design risk

Treat the page as a navigable evidence instrument rather than a conventional landing page. A single visual route should cross sections and change meaning as it moves from document, to event, to rule. The rest of the system stays quiet so the route remains memorable.
