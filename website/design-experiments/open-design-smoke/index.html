Start with the record.

Review the facts and terms attached to a general import record before moving beyond it.

Review requirements

Review the record in three parts.

01

Billing facts

Amounts, dates, currencies, charges, and parties shown in the record.

02

Operational facts

Shipment, handoff, timing, location, and handling details present in the record.

03

Governing terms and current rules

Terms supplied with the record and current requirements relevant to the review context.

Keep the review bounded.

This page organizes evidence for review. It does not state a legal conclusion or determine an outcome.
