Record reconstruction preview

Before you assess the charge, reconstruct the record.

A preview for finance and logistics teams tracing what belongs in the record before review.

See what to gather

Three records. One passage.

Keep each layer distinct, then place them in a sequence a qualified reviewer can examine.

  1. Billing record

    The charge as billed, with its references, dates, and stated basis.

  2. Operational record

    The movement and handling events connected to the billed period.

  3. Governing record

    The published terms and official regulatory sources selected for human review.

Gather in sequence.

Do not resolve the charge while the record is still incomplete.

  1. Gather the billing record

    Keep the complete record together, including the page where the charge appears.

  2. Gather the operational record

    Place dated movement and handling events in order without resolving conflicts.

  3. Gather the governing record

    Keep published terms beside the official source used for context.

  4. Mark what remains open

    Note missing pages, mismatched references, and unresolved dates before assessment.

Qualified human review.
