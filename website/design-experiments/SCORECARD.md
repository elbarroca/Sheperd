## A — Evidence Tide

- **Message clarity, 22/25:** the thesis is immediate and plain; the following `Three records. One passage.` line explains the mechanism. `Assess the charge` is slightly more abstract than Direction B's invoice-specific opening.
- **Audience relevance, 12/15:** the first viewport names finance and logistics teams but omits the approved `importer` qualifier.
- **Memorability, 12/15:** one route line changing from edge to timeline to underline is coherent and calm, but the sparse composition is less ownable than the annotated margins or terminal ledger.
- **Trust/evidence safety, 15/15:** three generic records, an explicit qualified-human stop, no collection, no verdict, and only official links. Static scan found one `h1`, zero forms/fields, and zero external runtime requests.
- **CTA, 9/10:** `See what to gather` accurately lands on the sequence; the lower close has no repeated action.
- **Mobile, 9/10:** the 375px capture reads cleanly in one column, and the 320px audit found no horizontal overflow.
- **Accessibility, 5/5:** Axe found zero violations at 320px; semantic landmarks, reduced motion, skip link, and visible focus are present.
- **Performance, 5/5:** self-contained 19.6 KB HTML, system fonts, code-native route, no external runtime assets or requests.

Evidence: `direction-a-evidence-tide/index.html`, `DESIGN.md`, `mobile-375.png`, `desktop-1440.png`.

## B — Margin Notes

- **Message clarity, 24/25:** `A D&D invoice is one record. The review needs the rest.` makes the missing-context problem concrete in one glance. The support line and CTA explain the next step without implying a verdict.
- **Audience relevance, 13/15:** D&D, invoice, source-date, and operational-record language fits importer finance and logistics work. The hero does not yet state the audience explicitly; that is the first refinement target.
- **Memorability, 15/15:** the case-file reading column, cobalt annotations, moving margin marker, and deliberately visible gaps form a distinct visual and verbal system without resembling a dashboard.
- **Trust/evidence safety, 15/15:** margin notes qualify every layer, the sequence stops at unresolved evidence, and the page explicitly states no collection and no legal conclusion. Static scan found one `h1`, zero forms/fields, and zero external runtime requests.
- **CTA, 10/10:** `Build the checklist` is concrete, on-page, repeated only at the finished close, and does not promise a SheperD service.
- **Mobile, 8/10:** the 375px page is readable and the 320px audit found no overflow, but the progress rail appears after the evidence sequence on mobile and the page is the longest of the three. Both are refinement targets.
- **Accessibility, 5/5:** Axe found zero violations at 320px; targets measured at 44px minimum, one `h1`, reduced-motion behavior, skip link, and semantic source links are present.
- **Performance, 5/5:** self-contained 27.2 KB HTML, system fonts, CSS paper texture, transform-only marker, and zero external runtime requests.

Evidence: `direction-b-margin-notes/index.html`, `DESIGN.md`, `mobile-375.png`, `desktop-1440.png`.

## C — Terminal Ledger

- **Message clarity, 21/25:** `Before the claim, align the record.` is compact and the audience/scope/stop register helps, but `align` requires more interpretation than Direction B's invoice/record contrast.
- **Audience relevance, 15/15:** the first viewport explicitly names importer finance and logistics teams and uses a manifest/terminal visual rhythm appropriate to the working context.
- **Memorability, 15/15:** condensed type, raw-paper rules, safety-yellow stops, and registration marks are the boldest identity route.
- **Trust/evidence safety, 15/15:** the preview boundary, `alignment is not agreement` language, qualified-human stop, and official-source-only section prevent a verdict implication. Static scan found one `h1`, zero forms/fields, and zero external runtime requests.
- **CTA, 8/10:** `Check the record` is concise but less specific than building or viewing a checklist.
- **Mobile, 8/10:** no 320px overflow and strong touch targets, but condensed uppercase copy and dense ruled modules demand more effort on a small screen.
- **Accessibility, 5/5:** Axe found zero violations at 320px; one `h1`, 44px targets, reduced-motion behavior, semantic reading order, and visible focus are present.
- **Performance, 5/5:** self-contained 27.4 KB HTML, code-native marks, system fonts, transform-only motion, and zero external runtime requests.

Evidence: `direction-c-terminal-ledger/index.html`, `DESIGN.md`, `mobile-375.png`, `desktop-1440.png`.
