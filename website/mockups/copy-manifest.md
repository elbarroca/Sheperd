## Form fields

`Name` · `Work email` · `Company` · `Role` · `Annual volume` · `Review context` · consent checkbox.

## Form states

- `DISABLED PREVIEW` — Submit is disabled until required fields are complete.
- `VALIDATION ERROR` — Enter a work email and company before continuing.
- `SUBMISSION FAILURE` — We couldn't send your request. Try again; no invoice upload is enabled here.

## Rejected alternatives

- `Before you assess the charge, reconstruct the record.` — clearer as editorial method copy, less direct financial value.
- `The invoice is only the first record.` — selected for the problem section, rejected as the financial hero.
- `Turn scattered shipment records into a reviewed next step.` — useful control-led support line, too abstract for the hero.
