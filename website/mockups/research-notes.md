## Research method

The supplied 1Password and 11x Refero style pages were read as design-system references. The current SheperD site was requested for live inspection; its browser fetch returned an internal error, so no live-site finding was invented. The SheperD direction was taken from the pasted brief and the locally supplied visual materials. Eight additional current premium sites were reviewed through live page text and rendered design evidence where available.

## Extracted design rules

1. Start in midnight navy, then move into paper and ice so the evidence becomes visible.
2. Use one electric-blue route across the story. It is a narrative signal, not decoration.
3. Let a high-contrast serif carry the financial tension; use a neutral sans for explanation and mono for metadata.
4. Use one major surface per section. Prefer borders and spacing over card stacks and shadows.
5. Keep synthetic artifacts tactile and obviously labelled. Never use fake logos, customer data, metrics, or proof.
6. Keep the only primary conversion action visible: `Request a pilot`.
7. Place `HUMAN REVIEW BOUNDARY` before `Potential credit or refund` in every outcome-adjacent visual.
8. Use amber only for missing evidence or conditional review.

## Audience and conversion

The audience is CFOs, controllers, AP leaders, supply-chain executives, logistics leaders, and import-operations owners at U.S. importers. The five-second job is: understand that SheperD reviews D&D invoices with surrounding records, see the case-specific boundary, and request a pilot.

Story sequence:

`invoice → billing record → operational event → governing term → human review → potential credit or refund`

The page uses one repeated CTA label and one repeated boundary line. Secondary navigation and `See the recovery path` support comprehension but do not compete with the pilot request.

## Copy territories

| Territory | Strength | Weakness | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome-led | Fastest financial comprehension; strongest CFO relevance. | Needs immediate case-specific boundary. | Selected. |
| Record-led | Highest trust and specificity; strongest evidence story. | Less immediate financial value at thumbnail scale. | Used as the section narrative. |
| Control-led | Strong operational ownership and process clarity. | Can sound like a software-control claim if overbuilt. | Used in method language, not as the hero. |

## Selected visual direction

**Evidence-to-recovery control room.** A dark, disciplined hero contains one synthetic invoice and one highlighted line item. The route crosses the invoice, then continues through billing, operational, and governing records. It ends at a visible human-review boundary before the potential credit/refund outcome. Light sections become paper-like evidence fields; one dark-teal band carries the process story.
