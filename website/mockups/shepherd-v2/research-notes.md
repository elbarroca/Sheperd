## Extracted landing-page logic

1. Announcement strip creates a current reason to continue.
2. Compact navigation keeps one clear CTA.
3. Centered hero states audience and promise before the visual proof.
4. The visual proof carries the product story without a dense explanation.
5. Capability sections use a repeated rhythm while changing the visual treatment.
6. A comparison section clarifies the difference in one scan.
7. A success section makes the service outcome explicit.
8. The footer closes with a direct navigation and trust boundary.

## SheperD adaptation

- The product visual becomes an original container-terminal film still.
- Product capabilities become billing records, operational events, and governing terms.
- The comparison becomes invoice-only view versus evidence-backed review.
- The success section states what a review can support and what remains unresolved.
- No copied logo, font, copy, screenshot, or product UI is used.
