# Rejected directions

## Tactile invoice hero

Rejected after review. It made SheperD feel like an invoice-processing tool and hid the physical D&D context. The invoice remains an evidence layer, not the hero subject.

## Generic dark SaaS split

Rejected. A left-copy, right-dashboard composition made the page look like a software template and weakened the importer story.

## Chosen direction

Container film outside. Evidence route inside.

The container terminal supplies the cinematic first impression. The page then moves inward through the charge, event, governing term, and human-review boundary.
