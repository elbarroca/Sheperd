# Rejected directions

## Hero explorations

### 01 — Recovery ledger

Large synthetic invoice on a dark field with the route crossing the highlighted line item. Strong artifact credibility, but the invoice dominated the first impression and reduced the financial headline’s breathing room.

### 02 — Cinematic evidence

Full-bleed generated material study with editorial type and a quiet endpoint. Most atmospheric, but the charge and pilot action became less explicit at thumbnail scale.

### 03 — Invoice-to-review

Selected. It puts the financial outcome and audience on the left, while the synthetic invoice, route, and human-review boundary carry the proof on the right. It scores best on five-second comprehension, CFO relevance, and conversion clarity.

## Rejected system directions

- Generic SaaS hero + three feature cards: too familiar; collapses the evidence story into interchangeable marketing blocks.
- Dashboard-first control room: rejected because fake metrics, fake status, and invented product UI would imply unsupported capability.
- Live port or container-yard photography: rejected because it implies a customer shipment, real event, or logistics proof.
- Neon AI / fintech gradient system: rejected because it creates urgency without evidence and weakens financial seriousness.
- Dense flowchart: rejected because the route should read as a visual thread, not require a legend.
- Logo wall / testimonial strip: rejected because no permissioned proof was supplied.
- Prior clean-room Preview identity: rejected as the controlling direction for this run because the pasted brief explicitly authorizes a new pilot-oriented rebrand.
