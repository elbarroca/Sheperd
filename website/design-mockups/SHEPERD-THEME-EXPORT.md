## Shared production rules

- Display serif plus one neutral grotesk; no third typeface.
- 12-column grid; 1,280px maximum content width.
- 112px desktop section rhythm; 72px tablet; 48px mobile.
- One narrative container per section; no card-inside-card nesting.
- Borders and spacing before shadows; shadows only on focal product surfaces.
- Blue is reserved for hierarchy, active state, evidence rails, and CTA.
- Brass appears only for a missing-evidence or gap state.
- Primary CTA: `See what to gather`.
- Boundary: `Educational Preview · No uploads · No case decision`.
- Footer: `SheperD · Preview only · No information is collected`.
