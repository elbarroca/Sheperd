# Ricardo — Strategic Interpretation

> [!important] Interpretation boundary
> This is Ricardo's analysis for Michael and the founders. It is not a verified company fact, legal advice, customer evidence, or authorization to execute. Each note points back to canonical evidence.

## Overall reading

Avi's proposal correctly recognizes that Michael's role is broader than sales: learn the domain, structure founder knowledge, build the commercial system, then lead controlled market learning. The strategic direction is useful. The timing and certainty language outrun the admitted authority, product, claims, security, capacity, and market evidence.

## What the proposal gets right

- Michael should progress from learner to builder to operating lead.
- Domain, value proposition, CRM, sales infrastructure, digital presence, segmentation, governance, and live learning must advance together.
- Customer conversations should refine segment, persona, message, partner, and business-model hypotheses.
- CRM discipline and weekly decisions are necessary to avoid anecdotal strategy.
- The website should reflect real customer learning rather than unsupported marketing claims.

## Corrections required before treating it as an operating commitment

1. The heading says seven axes but lists eight; Live Sales Execution is a distinct eighth axis.
2. Starting work before written engagement creates authority, confidentiality, IP, compensation, systems, and data-handling risk.
3. Fifty touches, 15–20 meetings, 3–5 submissions, and 3–5 deals are unsupported stretch assumptions, not an adopted funnel.
4. “Fluent,” “expert,” “proven,” “bulletproof,” “lead-gen machine,” and “scale-ready” need measurable definitions and sufficient evidence.
5. A two-year CRM should not be frozen in Week 2; ship an MVP and version it after Weeks 6 and 12.
6. Publishing, outreach, CRM intake, and customer-data submission require separate permission, claim, security, reviewer, and capacity gates.

## Implications for Michael

### Prepare now

- Complete founder, entity, role, product, claim, commercial, security, and capacity truth tables.
- Build internal talk tracks, objection taxonomy, CRM schema, partner criteria, scorecards, and decision cadence.
- Run deterministic claim and CRM-control tests only on synthetic records.
- Maintain a versioned record of assumptions, contradictions, and founder decisions.

### Execute only after approval

- Warm, cold, partner, inbound, website, and content cohorts.
- CRM records containing contact or permission data.
- Customer-data requests, receipt, analysis, recovery discussion, or case action.
- External claims, case studies, pricing, partner terms, or public publishing.

## Recommended founder conversation

Do not debate the ambition of the 16-week vision. Convert it into an evidence contract:

- What must be true before Michael represents SheperD?
- What exists today versus roadmap?
- Which exact claims and commercial terms are approved?
- Which system may hold each data class?
- Who reviews domain meaning and product output, within what SLA?
- Which Week 2 evidence permits the first bounded cohort?
- Which result causes continue, change, stop, or no-go?
