# Live Sales Execution

## Outcome

Controlled customer and partner conversations generate comparable learning and qualified commercial progress.

## Activation gate

- Written engagement and authority.
- Approved product/claims language.
- CRM MVP and cohort tags.
- Secure data path.
- Product/data and domain-review capacity.

## Next actions after gate

- [ ] Run a bounded warm-intro cohort.
- [ ] Review every outbound message before send.
- [ ] Capture discovery, objections, roles, evidence readiness, and next step.
- [ ] Retrospect at Week 6 before increasing volume.

## Measure

Activity quality, positive response, meeting held, qualification, valid submission, opportunity, outcome, and cycle time—reported with sample size.
