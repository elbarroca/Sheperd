# Technology and CRM

## Outcome

Reliable commercial data, secure handoffs, and minimal automation that improves decisions without hiding errors.

## Next actions

- [ ] Choose the CRM and create the MVP objects in [[04_Operations/CRM Data Model]].
- [ ] Define required fields and stage entry/exit criteria.
- [ ] Define secure data storage and metadata-only CRM links.
- [ ] Establish product/data response capacity and SLA.
- [ ] Pilot CRM hygiene assistance on 5–10 records.

## Measure

≥95% required-field completeness, zero raw customer data in Obsidian, stale-opportunity count, and submission-to-review time.
