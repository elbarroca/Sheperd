# Operating Cadence and KPI Dictionary

## Cadence

- **Daily async:** progress, blockers, next actions.
- **Monday — 30 min:** priorities, capacity, one active commercial hypothesis.
- **Wednesday — 20 min:** claim/domain questions and urgent blockers.
- **Friday — 45 min:** pipeline movement, cohorts, stale deals, approvals.
- **Biweekly — 60 min:** sprint retrospective and continue/change/stop decision.
- **Monthly — 60 min:** founder + commercial + product/data + domain/legal risk review.
- **Weeks 2, 6, 12, 16:** formal gates in [[03_GTM/16-Week Operating Plan]].

## Leading indicators

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Contactable-account rate | Valid, sourced accounts ÷ accounts received |
| Warm-intro acceptance | Introductions accepted ÷ introductions requested |
| Positive-response rate | Positive replies ÷ delivered touches, segmented by cohort |
| Meeting-held rate | Meetings held ÷ meetings booked |
| Qualified-meeting rate | Meetings meeting explicit criteria ÷ meetings held |
| Discovery completeness | Required discovery fields completed ÷ required fields |
| Data-request acceptance | Accepted data requests ÷ qualified meetings |
| Valid-submission rate | Product/data-accepted submissions ÷ accepted requests |
| CRM completeness | Records with owner, source, stage, next action/date, outcome reason ÷ active records; target ≥95% |
| Stale opportunities | Active opportunities without activity or next step for 14 days |
| Partner quality | Accepted qualified referrals ÷ partner referrals |
| Experiment discipline | One documented hypothesis and decision per sprint |

## Qualification pass/fail rules

| Event | Pass | Fail or excluded |
|---|---|---|
| Positive reply | A human response acknowledges a relevant D&D problem, requests information, accepts an introduction/meeting, or proposes a concrete next step. | Auto-replies, bounces, unsubscribes, generic courtesy, referral without acceptance, explicit no, or unrelated response. |
| Qualified meeting | Meeting was held; account fit, pain, responsible stakeholder, available evidence, timing, authority path, and next step are recorded; no disqualifier is unresolved. | Booked/no-show only, required fields missing, no relevant problem/evidence path, or explicit disqualifier. |
| Qualified opportunity | A qualified meeting plus a plausible case-specific path to value, named owner, secure-data path, agreed next action/date, and product/data-review capacity. | Interest without owner, evidence path, secure handoff, next step, or review capacity. |
| Accepted data request | Prospect confirms the defined checklist, approved secure method, responsible owner, and intended submission date. | Request sent only, verbal interest without owner/date, email attachment plan, or unapproved transfer method. |
| Valid submission | Product/data owner marks the package complete enough for the agreed analysis using a versioned checklist and records acceptance date. | Received but pending review, incomplete, unsafe, unauthorized, duplicate, or rejected with reason. |
| Closed won | Authorized parties execute the governing commercial agreement and the start/handoff condition is met; booked value follows the approved finance rule. | Verbal yes, proposal sent, pilot interest, unsigned agreement, or recovery expectation without contract. |

These rules are proposed until Avi, product/data, and legal/commercial owners approve them before Week 2 activation.

## Lagging indicators

- Valid data submissions.
- Qualified opportunities and stage conversion.
- Proposal count/value and stage-weighted pipeline.
- Closed-won count, contracted revenue, and expected gross margin.
- Median sales-cycle duration.
- Win/loss/no-decision reasons.
- Submission-to-result and result-to-recovery time.
- Realized customer outcome, retention, expansion, and referrals when mature enough.

## Mandatory segmentation

Every rate must be sliceable by warm/cold, account segment, persona, channel, message version, partner, date cohort, carrier/port hypothesis, and source. Never compare blended rates when cohort mix changed.

## Metric governance

- Define numerator, denominator, event time, owner, and exclusions before reporting.
- Report sample size beside percentages.
- Do not call small-sample differences statistically reliable.
- Preserve lost and no-decision records; they are learning, not cleanup.
